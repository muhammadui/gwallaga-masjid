# Gwallaga Juma'at Masjid — Discovery Research

Date: 25 September 2026. Status: research, not a spec. Planning follows this.

## 1. The masjid (what is public)

**Confirmed**
- Gwallaga Juma'at / Central Mosque, Gwallaga ward, Bauchi city, on Murtala Muhammad Way near Wunti Road. Hausa-speaking Sunni congregation. Women's section, wudu block, parking.
- It is the **Bauchi State secretariat of JIBWIS (Izala)**. Hosted JIBWIS national preaching (March 2024) with the governor, Dr Abdullahi Bala Lau and Sheikh Kabiru Gombe.
- **Nationally known for the annual Ramadan tafsir of Prof. Muhammad Sani Umar Rijiyar Lemo.** Episodes are scattered across YouTube, darulhadithonline.com and archive.org. Nobody owns this archive digitally.
- Runs an **orphans' welfare committee** coordinating 18 neighbourhood units (2,100 orphans served, Sallah 2026).
- Former Chief Imam Ibrahim Idris died November 2024. Successor not found online.
- **No website, app or verified social account exists.** Only auto-generated Facebook place pages and directory listings. Greenfield.

**Not public / do not search for**
- The WAKF reconstruction, its cost, commissioning date, capacity, architect. Offline initiative. We get these from the team directly.

**Implications**
- Doctrinal identity is Salafi/Izala: no Maulud, zikr or wird content. Tone is scholarly, sober, warm.
- The tafsir archive is the crown jewel. It is the one thing that makes Gwallaga a national name, and it is currently homeless.
- We define the brand from zero. There is no legacy to respect except the building and the scholars.

## 2. What a great Juma'at masjid offers (catalogue)

Grouped from East London Mosque, Green Lane, Mawaqit, Mohid, Masjidal, NASFAT and Northern Nigeria sources.

- **Worship**: five prayers with adhan + iqamah, jumu'ah (often two sittings), tarawih/qiyam, last-10-nights, i'tikaf sign-up, Eid, janazah notices, istisqa'. Digital: imam-set timetable, iqamah countdown, push reminders, Ramadan sehri/iftar calendar, Hijri date, live + archived khutbah, "janazah today" alerts.
- **Education**: tahfiz with hifz tracking, tajweed, tafsir circles, hadith circles, Maliki fiqh texts (Risala, Akhdari, Mukhtasar Khalil), Arabic, Islamiyya classes 1–6, women's halaqa, youth, new-Muslim classes, pre-marriage course. Digital: registration and fees, parent portal, lecture library, podcast RSS.
- **Life services**: nikah + certificate, walima hall, pre-marital and family counselling, sulh mediation, suna (naming, day 7), aqiqah, janazah (ghusl, kafan, burial), shahada + certificate, Ask-the-Imam, inheritance calculation, hospital visits. Digital: booking forms, appointment slots, funeral hotline, fatwa archive.
- **Welfare**: zakat collection and 8-category distribution, zakat al-fitr, sadaqah, waqf, Ramadan iftar, food bank, orphans/widows, medical outreach, scholarships, qard hasan, udhiyah. Digital: zakat calculator with nisab, campaign pages, recurring giving, beneficiary forms, receipts, published distribution reports.
- **Community**: announcements, events, guest scholars, volunteers, youth, women's committee, library, guest rooms, skills training, blood drives. Digital: push, feed, RSVP, rostering, verse/hadith of the day.
- **Facilities**: wudu, women's entrance, accessibility, Friday parking, halls for hire, lost and found, borehole, solar, security, waqf shops. Digital: hall booking, facility map, opening hours.
- **Hajj/Umrah**: manasik classes, Bauchi Pilgrims Welfare Board registration and deposits (₦7m+ for 2027), airlift schedules.
- **Administration**: imam/khatib rota, khutbah archive, live streaming, membership, donor records, published financials, trustees, staff, asset/waqf register.

**Northern Nigeria specifics**
- Ramadan tafsir is the flagship programme; numbered daily episodes are the natural archive unit.
- Islamiyya (structured, fee-paying) vs Tsangaya/almajiri (boarding, begging). Masjid welfare means almajiri feeding, clothing, health.
- Maliki fiqh; Zauren Sulh and the Bauchi Sharia Commission handle family disputes. Masjid zakat is informal and cash-based, so transparency reports build trust.
- Rites: suna on day 7 with ram; nikah in the masjid with sadaki announced; same-day burial.
- Language: Hausa first (Latin script), Arabic liturgy, English second. Gumi's Hausa Quran translation is standard.
- Money: USSD and wallets (OPay *955#, PalmPay *861#, Moniepoint), bank transfer, cash box. Show account numbers and transfer memo codes, not card-only forms.
- Channels: WhatsApp reaches 96%+ of Nigerian internet users. Broadcast lists and Channels are the announcement system. Facebook Live/YouTube for tafsir.
- Devices: affordability; feature-phone resurgence; data is expensive. Text-first, audio over video, downloadable MP3, SMS/USSD fallback, offline timetable.

## 3. What "great" looks like (product and design survey)

**Feature leaders**
- Cambridge Central Mosque: only mosque site with date (Gregorian + Hijri), Jumu'ah times and a Begins/Jama'at grid above the fold; architecture story as identity.
- Green Lane Masjid: best donation copy ("67p a day"), named khatib next to Jumu'ah time, GLM TV and podcasts.
- Haramain / Nusuk: calm minimalist UI over huge complexity; live sermons with translations; 10 languages.
- LaunchGood: best giving UX. Quick Donate, Ramadan Challenge (auto-give nightly at local Maghrib, boost last 10), Give List.
- NZF UK: donation form is the hero. Zakat / Sadaqah / Fidyah fields, then Give.
- Islamic Relief: calculator hands its exact result into checkout.
- Pillars app: serif display, restrained palette, no ads, lock-screen next prayer, streaks. The closest thing to a premium prayer UI.
- Quran.com / Quran Foundation: gold-standard reader, word-by-word, layered translations.
- Mohid / Masjidal / MasjidBox: deepest ops (kiosk, membership, room scheduler, screens), visually dated.

**Design references (premium, cultural, Gulf/heritage)**
1. experiencealula.com: full-bleed hero, interest filter ("Show me everything / Heritage / Wellness"), priced event cards.
2. mia.org.qa (Museum of Islamic Art): quiet 4-up grid, editorial captions, consistent image ratios.
3. ithra.com: Visit / Programs / Join / Membership IA, magazine as content brand.
4. cambridgecentralmosque.org: prayer-first hierarchy, eco-mosque story.
5. Virtual Expo Dubai 2020 (Dogstudio, Awwwards SOTD): two-colour palette, Three.js, menu as a set piece.
6. Reviving Saudi History (DARAH, Awwwards): WebGL scroll storytelling of heritage.
7. Diriyah: Webby best visual design; Najdi earth palette, heritage photography.
8. Baltic / Nordiska Museet: typographic load animation, open/closed status in header, colour per exhibition.
9. nzf.org.uk: zero-click path to giving.
10. Pillars: see above. Honourable: Foster + Partners, Snøhetta, BIG, HAQQ.

**Patterns to steal**
- Prayer strip above the fold, server-rendered HTML, next prayer highlighted with a countdown.
- Bilingual type as a relationship: Latin serif/sans paired with an Arabic face of matching weight; Arabic set 10–15% larger optically.
- Restrained palette plus one accent. Earth, limestone, Najdi ochre. Not green-white-gold arabesque.
- Calm architecture photography, consistent crops, geometric masks, full-bleed hero video.
- Motion on the shell (type-led load, pinned hero, section colour shifts), never on the timetable.
- Status line: "Jumu'ah in 2h 14m".
- Giving: presets + per-day reframing, one-tap wallets, Maghrib-keyed scheduled giving, labelled Zakat/Sadaqah fields, calculator into checkout.
- Hijri first-class: header date, Ramadan and Eid landing pages, religious days.
- Khutbah archive as a media brand with named playlists, /live and /live-audio pages.
- Visitor module: tour slots, mosque manners, plan your visit.
- Admin sets times once, pushed everywhere (site, app, screens).

**Weaknesses to avoid**
- Prayer times buried under a news carousel or in a PDF. Calculated times that ignore actual iqamah.
- WordPress mosque-theme tells: stock slider, gmail contact, icon soup. Most Nigerian mosques have no site at all; Abuja National Mosque is a WordPress theme.
- Government-portal UI, 30-link mega-menus, ad-funded apps, dark-pattern trials.
- Mismatched Arabic fonts, RTL as afterthought, SPA-only rendering that breaks low-bandwidth users and SEO.
- No Hijri, no next-prayer state, no live/archive continuity, no Ramadan mode.

## 6. Synthesis: the direction I recommend

**Concept.** Not a "mosque website". A digital house for the masjid with three faces:
1. **The House** — the building, its story, the renovation, the people. This is where the awwwards feel lives: cinematic, typographic, calm.
2. **The Minbar** — live and archive. The Rijiyar Lemo tafsir seasons become the most complete tafsir library in Nigeria, audio-first and shareable on WhatsApp. This is what makes the platform matter beyond Bauchi.
3. **The Service Desk** — prayer, giving, nikah, welfare. Fast, HTML-first, works on ₦500 of data.

**Design posture.** Premium through restraint: limestone/earth palette with one accent, Arabic and Latin type as a matched pair, calm photography of the real building, GSAP choreography on the shell only. The timetable and the donate button never animate.

**Progressive enhancement.** Two tiers of experience from one codebase: full cinematic on good connections, text-and-audio on slow ones. Detected via saveData and effectiveType, not a "lite" toggle.

**Surfaces.** Public site + installable PWA (push, offline timetable) + admin panel (times, Hijri offset, streams, bookings, donations, content). Native app is a later question.

## 7. Risks and open items
- Hijri and moon-sighting divergence: admin override is mandatory, never compute Eid.
- Paystack dedicated accounts need a CAC-registered entity. Whose entity: the masjid, JIBWIS Bauchi, or WAKF?
- Nikah certificate legal standing: masjid-issued, imam-signed. Confirm relationship with Sharia court registration.
- Rights and sources for existing tafsir recordings. Who holds the masters?
- WhatsApp template approval lead time.
- Current Chief Imam and committee names needed from the team.
- Brand assets: renders, drone footage, photography of the new building, logo. The premium feel depends on this more than on code.
